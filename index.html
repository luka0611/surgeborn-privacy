Surgeborn - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10/15/2026

Surgeborn is a game made by Lukas Silva Rocha. This page explains what the game stores and why.

What we collect

  • A player ID. When you play online features, the game signs you in anonymously with Unity Gaming Services and gets a random player ID. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your game progress. Your save (level, quests, items, looks) is stored on your device and, for cloud saves, on Unity Gaming Services, linked to that player ID.
  • Purchases. If you buy Shards or packs, Google Play handles the payment. We receive a purchase record (what was bought and when) to deliver it. We never see your card or bank details.
  • Crash and performance reports. If the game crashes or hits an error, it sends a report (the error, the phone model, the OS version, memory) to Unity Diagnostics so we can fix it.
  • Online co-op. When you play with friends, the game sends your character's position, animation and hits to the other players through Unity's relay servers. Those servers see your internet (IP) address to connect you. There is no text or voice chat.

What we do not do

  • No ads and no advertising ID.
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone for advertising.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or messages.

Who processes the data

Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services: sign-in, cloud save, multiplayer relay) and Google (Google Play purchases) process data on our behalf to run these features. Their privacy policies: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy and https://policies.google.com/privacy

Security

Data sent between the game and these services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS / TLS).

Deleting your data

In the game: Settings > "Delete my online data" removes your online player data and cloud save. Or email us at zjenanplays@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and your in-game player name; we delete it within 30 days. Uninstalling the game removes the data stored on your device.

Children

Surgeborn is meant for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
